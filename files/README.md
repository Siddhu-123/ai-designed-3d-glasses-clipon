# Example files (reference only)

These are from my build and fit **my** glasses (Specsavers Dylan+ 57□20-145, steel front, ×1.011 measured scale). Do not print them for other glasses.

- `clipon_v15_1_example.stl`: the final clip, light copy (25,000 triangles, within 0.019 mm of the print file). Use it to look at the design, not to print.
- `film_template_example_A4.pdf`: 1:1 film cut template. Print at 100 % and check that the 50 mm bar measures 50 mm.
